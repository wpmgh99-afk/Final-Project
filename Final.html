2008 Beijing Olympics - Final

The 2008 Beijing Olympics Men's Singles final was Lin Dan's ultimate statement to the world. Playing on his home court in China, "Super Dan" was virtually untouchable. He overwhelmed Lee Chong Wei with aggressive net play and explosive smashes, winning definitively in straight sets (21-12, 21-8). This victory cemented his status as a global superstar.

2011 World Championships - Final

Held at Wembley Arena in London, this match is often considered a masterpiece of the sport. The two legends pushed each other to their absolute physical and mental limits. After saving match points in an incredibly tense third set, Lin Dan narrowly defeated Lee Chong Wei (20-22, 21-14, 23-21) to secure his fourth World Championship title in a true classic.

2012 London Olympics - Final

Arguably the most famous match in badminton history. Lee Chong Wei was desperate for Olympic gold, and he pushed Lin Dan to a nail-biting 79-minute thriller. In an intense third set, Lin Dan secured the gold medal on the final two points (15-21, 21-10, 21-19), becoming the first man to win back-to-back Olympic singles titles.

2016 Rio Olympics - Semi-Final

In a match touted as the "Real Final," Lee Chong Wei finally broke his Olympic curse against Lin Dan. In another three-set thriller, Lee Chong Wei saved his best for last, defeating his greatest rival (15-21, 21-11, 22-20). The match ended with an iconic, emotional hug and jersey swap between the two warriors, marking the end of their Olympic rivalry.
