Famous Races

The greatest rivalries and legendary clashes in badminton history

Click any match image to watch the highlights

2008 Beijing Olympics - Final

The 2008 Beijing Olympics Men's Singles final was Lin Dan's ultimate statement to the world. Playing on his home court in China, "Super Dan" was virtually untouchable. He overwhelmed Lee Chong Wei with aggressive net play and explosive smashes, winning definitively in straight sets (21-12, 21-8). This victory cemented his status as a global superstar.

2011 World Championships - Final

Held at Wembley Arena in London, this match is often considered a masterpiece of the sport. The two legends pushed each other to their absolute physical and mental limits. After saving match points in an incredibly tense third set, Lin Dan narrowly defeated Lee Chong Wei (20-22, 21-14, 23-21) to secure his fourth World Championship title in a true classic.

2012 London Olympics - Final

Arguably the most famous match in badminton history. Lee Chong Wei was desperate for Olympic gold, and he pushed Lin Dan to a nail-biting 79-minute thriller. In an intense third set, Lin Dan secured the gold medal on the final two points (15-21, 21-10, 21-19), becoming the first man to win back-to-back Olympic singles titles.

2016 Rio Olympics - Semi-Final

In a match touted as the "Real Final," Lee Chong Wei finally broke his Olympic curse against Lin Dan. In another three-set thriller, Lee Chong Wei saved his best for last, defeating his greatest rival (15-21, 21-11, 22-20). The match ended with an iconic, emotional hug and jersey swap between the two warriors, marking the end of their Olympic rivalry.

2006 Malaysia Open - Final

One of the greatest comebacks in badminton history. Playing on home soil, Lee Chong Wei found himself down 13-20 in the deciding third set. Facing seven match points against a prime Lin Dan, Lee staged a miraculous recovery, scoring eight consecutive points and eventually winning 23-21. This match solidified his legendary mental resilience.

2013 World Championships - Final

A dramatic and controversial clash in Guangzhou. After returning from nearly a year off, Lin Dan proved his genius by reaching the final. The match went to a grueling third set. Tragically, at 20-17 in Lin Dan's favor, Lee Chong Wei suffered severe leg cramps and was forced to retire, having to be stretchered off the court while Lin Dan checked on his friend.

2014 Asian Games - Semi-Final

Considered one of the highest-quality matches of their rivalry. Lin Dan and Lee Chong Wei traded breathtaking rallies in Incheon, South Korea. Lin Dan narrowly edged out the first set 22-20, Lee dominated the second 21-12, but Lin Dan found an extra gear in the third, winning it 21-9 to advance to the finals and defend his Asian Games gold.

2018 Malaysia Open - Final

A masterclass by the 35-year-old Malaysian King. Lee Chong Wei delighted his home crowd in Kuala Lumpur by defeating his greatest rival in straight sets (21-17, 23-21). This victory was particularly historic, as it marked Lee Chong Wei's 12th Malaysia Open title, an unbelievable record that cemented his legacy on home soil.

2019 Malaysia Open - The Reunion

While they didn't play each other, this was one of the most emotional moments in badminton history. Lin Dan won the tournament by defeating Chen Long in the final. To the crowd's surprise, Lee Chong Wei—who was sidelined recovering from cancer treatment—walked out to personally present the trophy to Lin Dan, resulting in a beautiful, tear-jerking reunion between the two lifelong rivals.
